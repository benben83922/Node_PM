BigObject Inc., a technology innovator and a live data solution provider. With our core technology of **In-Data Computing**, we launched a flagship product - BigObject® Store that can quickly and continuously handle non-stop and all aspects data streams.

Our mission is “**Helping everyone and every company in the world to turn data into action**". We serve two  applications in smart business - BONATM and spatial-temporal artificial intelligence - STAITM .

Our solutions and services aim to enable enterprises to take advantage of all data in the real world to optimize business performance and to transform business into data-driven enterprises.
